---
description: Read the user's LoupeEdit notes now
---

Call the loupe `status()` tool now and answer every NOTE line it carries (the user's notes from the LoupeEdit window). If the loupe tools are missing: LoupeEdit runs on the user's own computer, inside the Claude desktop app's reach. If the loupe tools are missing, fail to connect, or answer E_LOCAL: stop and ask the user to open the Claude desktop app and LoupeEdit on their computer, then try again. Never rebuild the cut in your own workspace (no ffmpeg, no copies of their files there): every edit goes through the loupe tools, on their files, by their paths on their computer.
